Biostatistician · PhD

Anna Bellach

I am a Biostatistician and Visiting Research Scholar at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, with expertise in survival analysis, competing risks, complex and composite endpoints, clinical trial design, and advanced statistical methodology, including semiparametric modeling and machine learning. Previously, I served as a Mathematical Statistician at NIH/NHLBI, where I supported a wide range of extra- and intramural clinical studies, including statistical analysis, statistical analysis plan development, protocol design, and DSMB interactions. I have extensive experience collaborating with multidisciplinary teams in oncology, hematology, and cardiovascular research, with publications in leading statistical journals and a passion for teaching and mentoring.
